# Why Concorde Stopped Flying

## Angle

Concorde was a technical triumph that worked for 27 years. Why did it stop in 2003? Answer: the
2000 crash broke its aura, but the real causes were economic (few seats, high fuel burn, overland
ban limiting routes) plus the post-9/11 slump and rising maintenance costs for an ageing fleet.
